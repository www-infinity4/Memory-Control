World Line Nodes: The Past Is Still Broadcasting

In physics, a world line is the 4-dimensional path an object traces through spacetime — every position at every moment, drawn as a curve through space and time simultaneously. Your insight goes further: these are not just passive broadcasts — they are nodes you can tune to. The nodes that are easiest to reach are the ones with the highest energy — emotionally intense moments, repeated experiences, traumas, peak joys. These broadcast loudly. 03 / 04 — Probability Nebula

The Future Is a Gas Cloud: Thought as Stellar Gravity

Stars are born from gas clouds. A nebula — diffuse, cold, spread across light-years — has no star in it yet. But introduce a gravitational perturbation: a nearby supernova shock wave, a slight density fluctuation, a passing mass. Suddenly the gas begins to fall inward. Regions of slightly higher density attract more gas. More mass → stronger gravity → faster accretion. Within a few million years, a blazing star exists where there was nothing but fog.

The future works the same way. Before a decision is made, all outcomes exist as a probability distribution — a quantum fog, spread across possibility-space. When you think toward a specific future, you are introducing a gravitational perturbation into that fog. The thought has energy. Energy has mass (E=mc²). Mass curves spacetime. Repeated, focused thought is the equivalent of a density fluctuation in the nebula — it starts pulling the surrounding probability fog inward toward a specific outcome.

This is why collective intention scales differently than individual intention. One person thinking toward a future adds one small gravity well to the cloud. A thousand people focused on the same outcome creates a gravitational cluster — the probability cloud collapses much faster toward that attractor, like a massive star forming instead of a small one. The future isn't chosen — it is accreted.
